What is the TMGM Partners Program?

The TMGM Partners Program pays you for introducing traders to TMGM. There are four ways to do it: refer traders you know as an Introducing Broker, send traffic as an Affiliate, create content as an Influencer, or combine upfront CPA and rebates with Hybrid. Pick the one that fits how you already work.

Introducing Broker

For people who know traders personally.

  • How you earnRebates on every trade your referred clients make.
  • When you’re paidDaily, 365 paydays a year.
  • Key factRates are tiered and agreed with your partnership manager. Build a network with Sub-IBs.
Explore the program: Introducing Broker

Affiliate

For site owners, media buyers, publishers and channels.

  • How you earnCPA of up to US$600 per qualified client, tiered.
  • When you’re paidMonthly.
  • Key factEvery click, registration and funded account tracked in real time.
Explore the program: Affiliate

Influencer Partnership Program

For creators who publish posts, videos, webinars or live sessions.

  • How you earnA fixed fee for every post and activity you deliver, plus ongoing IB rebates.
  • When you’re paidIn stages, starting with 50% when your agreement is signed.
  • Key fact3, 6 or 12-month campaigns.
Explore the program: Influencer

Hybrid

For IBs and affiliates who want both models.

Available in selected countries
  • How you earnUpfront CPA plus ongoing trading rebates, in a 20/80, 50/50 or 80/20 split.
  • When you’re paidCPA when a referred client qualifies, plus rebates on their trading.
